Things to Do and Actions to Take

WHAT ACTIONS CAN YOU TAKE?

No need to start big, After all big changes starts with a small step.

Reduce Plastic Pollution

  • Use reusable shopping bags and water bottles.
  • Avoid single-use plastics, such as straws and plastic cutlery.
  • Separate recyclable waste from general rubbish.
  • Never throw rubbish into drains, rivers or the sea.

Participate in Beach and Coastal Cleanups

  • Join organised beach-cleanup activities.
  • Collect litter safely using gloves and appropriate equipment.
  • Encourage schools and communities to organise cleanup events.
  • Record and report pollution to the appropriate local authorities.

Protect Marine Animals and Coral Reefs

  • Never touch, stand on or break coral reefs.
  • Observe marine animals from a safe and respectful distance.
  • Avoid purchasing products made from threatened marine species.
  • Support marine conservation and habitat restoration projects.

Support Sustainable Fishing

  • Choose seafood from responsible and traceable sources when possible.
  • Follow fishing regulations, including size limits and seasonal restrictions.
  • Avoid buying illegally caught or threatened species.
  • Avoid buying illegally caught or threatened species.

Prevent Water Pollution

  • Dispose of chemicals, oils and medicines properly.
  • Never pour harmful substances into drains or waterways.
  • Reduce fertiliser and pesticide runoff where possible.
  • Report illegal dumping and sewage pollution.

Educate Others and Support Conservation

  • Share reliable information about ocean conservation.
  • Organise environmental awareness campaigns at school.
  • Support organisations working to protect marine ecosystems.
  • Encourage communities to reduce waste and protect local waterways.
